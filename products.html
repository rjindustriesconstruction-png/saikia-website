How it works

OUR PROCESS

1

Sourced from the river

Sand and gravel are taken from the river bed.

2

Screened & washed

The screener washes and separates sand and gravel into sizes.

3

Larger stone crushed

Soling and boulder go to the crusher for aggregates.

4

Graded & dispatched

Each product is loaded and sold by the cubic meter.
